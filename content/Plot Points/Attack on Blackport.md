# Synopsis
On the night of 23 Mutio, NL 223, [[Blackport]] was attacked by a coordinated group of [[Fiends]]. The creatures pushed into the fortress, spearheaded by a large worm and imposing worm, and scoured the town for a number of unknown articles leaving a wake of destruction behind. The titanic creature, identified as [[Möju]] destroyed the fortress, taking many lives in its crawl.

Throughout the event, the #players spend the night attempting to check in on various members or the city, and also drive back what fiends they can. After assembling a number of soldiers and holy faithful, the #players survive the night, only to realize that many things are missing.

[[Pinity Ironmountain|Pinity]], cousin to [[Trenton|Tyr Emeraldcrag]] has left the city during the night in search of something taken by the [[Fiends]]. The item she seeks is a [[Ritual Stones|Ritual Stone]], something that hold some significance to a rumor long held in [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] cultre.

[[Baldrick Stone]], after being rescued by [[Alex|Paolo Piranha]], speaks of some of the strange [[Ritual Stones]] he and his band had procured from the [[The Ash]] not long before for a merchant expedition. Scouring the market, no sign of these stone, held by either the local merchants, or strangely, some of the [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] craftsman remain. 

In another twist, in the wake of the fortress' destruction, an assassination plot is enacted in the royal palace back at [[Maelbreak]], leading to a palace investigator, [[Kethic Tamrin|Count Tamrin]] making his way to the [[Obsidian Fortress]]. Through intervention by the Party, a final truth is revealed, a plot by the [[Demons|Prime Demon]] [[Jaggeth]] is underway to reclaim the [[Ritual Stones]] stones for his resurrection. [[Kethic Tamrin|Kethic]] is an agent of this plot, and there is more to deal with in the capital city.

With all this information, the Party decides on a path, they choose to brave the terrible conditions of [[The Ash]] to search for [[Pinity Ironmountain|Pinity]], and learn more about the stolen [[Ritual Stones]].

[[The Expedition]] is born.
