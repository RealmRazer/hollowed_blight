# Description
Stone anchors creating after the Dawning. The stone is a intersection of knowledge between ancient Elven scholars and Dwarven craftsman. Stones are crafted with old lost elven magics and songs that have been thought best forgotten and use Deep Melcyite created by dwarves as a base.

Each stone is inscribed with writings and incantations on how to bind a specific creature to a master or location.
