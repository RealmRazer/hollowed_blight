# Description
An immense fiend spotted during the [[Attack on Blackport]]. Other fiends were seen running stones and artifacts from the city into it's mouth. During a recitation from [[Connor|Thalion]], it was referred to as a the "Right Arm of the King."

It holds a connection to [[Jaggeth]], in the [[Ritual Stones|Ritual Stone]] that lies at its heart. When [[The Expedition]] encountered the creature in the depths of [[Apstarris]], it was slain by the combined efforts of multiple armies and the Party.
