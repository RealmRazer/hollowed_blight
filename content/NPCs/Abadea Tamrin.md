# Background
A noble in kingdom of [[Pinem]], and father to [[Kethic Tamrin]]. He was discovered in deep underground in [[Bharn Darom]]. He was one of the figures present near the king of [[Otia]], but his exact intentions aren't known.

He was left in a state of near death by [[Connor|Thalion]] in his anger.
