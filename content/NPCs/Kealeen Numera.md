# Background
A saint from years past who traveled the lands trying to right a number of wrongs. Her final journey saw her trying to tackle demons in [[The Ash]] while traveling the lands of [[Racinica]]. After traveling the lands, she mysteriously disappeared.  During a vision, she revealed her final location to the center mountains of [[The Ash]].

Her disappearance sparked the creation of the [[Numeran Faith|Nemeran Faith]], a church of both good natured and corrupt individuals attempting to capitalize on her deeds.

[[The Expedition]] found her standing alone at the ends of the worlds during their confrontation with [[Jaggeth]]. Clutching the last [[Ritual Stones|Ritual Stone]], she assisted the party in banishing the [[Demons|Demon]], and was lost to the worlds once more after the collapse of the extradimensional space.
