# Background
A [[Beastfolk]] previously living in [[Lou'Toonie]]. He followed [[Blue|Jeremiah]] around to [[Blackport]]. but found himself as a servant and performer at [[Murcurio's Marvels]]. After the [[Attack on Blackport]], [[Blue|Jeremiah]] freed him and they've been traveling together ever since.

Though he is physically freed from [[Murcurio Vantir|Murcurio]], he is still under a legal contract.
