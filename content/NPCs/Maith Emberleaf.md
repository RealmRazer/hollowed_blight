# Background
An elder, teaching music at the citadel in [[Pramos]]. A minstrel from the Elven Citadel who taught [[Connor|Thalion]] during his youth. After a long journey and coming back to the Citadel, she departed this world started the journey West.

Her soul was brough back into the world by [[Thomas|Cor Devlin]] in [[Pavlon]] at the behest of [[Connor|Thalion]] in order to sort out the nature of his visions.
