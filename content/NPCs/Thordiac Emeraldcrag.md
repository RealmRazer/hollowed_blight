# Background


The patriarch of the [[Emeraldcrag]] family. He leads each disparate house as to a semblance of unity in [[Otia]]. He holds power in high regard and will use whatever is necessary to ensure that the family line prospers and stands tall, hopefully one day leading the entire [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] nation.
