# Background
A daughter of [[Thordiac Emeraldcrag]] and the Goldtor family. She is an old friend of [[Trenton|Tyr]] and one of the succession candidates for the [[Emeraldcrag]] throne. She is a kind overseer to the Goldtor household and concerned with unknown elements that seem to be happening behind the scene.
