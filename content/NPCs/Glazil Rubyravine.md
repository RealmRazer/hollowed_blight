# Background
A merchant managing trade runs between [[Otia]] and [[Racinica]]. [[The Expedition]] encountered him during their escape from [[Maelbreak]]. Carrying a shipment of steel to [[Baldor Emeraldcrag]], [[The Expedition]] stowed away on his ship to travel to [[Trord]].

He was promised an enterprising opportunity to open a new trade route to [[Pavlon]] that was being set up by [[Adam|Father Octavian]] and [[Trenton|Tyr]].
