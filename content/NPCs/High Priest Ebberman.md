# Background
A high ranking priest of the [[Numeran Faith|Numeran Faith]] and part of [[Pinity Ironmountain|Pinity's]] expedition into [[Pavlon]]. After reaching the city entrance, he saddled along with [[Adam|Father Octavian]] in order to secure the city for the church and to raise his own position.

A selfish leader of the faith who attempts to control anything possible to better his own standing.

After wandering alone and surviving the brunt of [[The Ash]], he reunited with the knights and nobles of [[Lantrik]] to reclaim [[Pavlon]]. After being stopped at the entrance of the city by [[Adam|Father Octavian]], in his exhausted state, he had his mind and will overwritten to more closely conform to the goals of [[Adam|Father Octavian]]; working to protect the city of [[Pavlon]] and it's current inhabitants.
