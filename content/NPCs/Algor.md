# Background
A mercenary who previously worked under the Golden Accord trade union. He worked as muscle, taking down threats to the trade union and also leading a number of mage slayers at the command of [[Kassim]].

After a bad hit on the docks, and the intervention of [[Thomas|Cor Devlin]], he was recruited to the Pretty Pirates.
