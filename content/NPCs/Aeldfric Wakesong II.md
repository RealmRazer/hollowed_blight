# Background
The prince of [[Pinem]] and son of [[Aeldfric Wakesong]]. An arrogant man who would do all within his power to take the reigns of power from his father and cement himself at the center of the world.

He has joined hands with [[Kethic Tamrin]] in order to overthrow the imperial family and cement his power.
