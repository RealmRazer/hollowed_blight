# Background
A high ranking priest of the [[Numera|Numeran Faith]] part of [[Pinity Ironmountain|Pinity's]] expedition into [[Pavlon]]]. After reaching the city entrance, he saddled along with [[Adam|Father Octavian]] in order to secure the city for the church and to raise his own position.

A selfish leader of the faith who attempts to control anything possible to better his own standing.
