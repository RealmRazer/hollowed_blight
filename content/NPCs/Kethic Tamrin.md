# Background
A noble and Count in the kingdom of [[Pinem]]. Kethic commands a small military battalions in addition to ruling a territory outside [[Maelbreak]] known as [[Carleigh]].

Due to the attacks that have took place in the capitol against aids to the royal family, [[Aeldfric Wakesong|King Aeldfric]] dispatched him to neighboring territories to route out as dissent in the kingdoms. After he arrived in [[Blackport]], he was lead astray by The Expedition due to his seeming demonic ties.

With the aid of [[Alden Blackwood|Duke Alden]] and a missive sent back to [[Maelbreak]], he was detained as part of an investigation into the turmoil in the capitol.

After his detention he has shown links to different elements of demonic worship being spread in the capitol. While presented as being on house arrest, he was seen walking the grounds of the guards quarters.
