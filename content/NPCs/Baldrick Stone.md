# Background
A mercenary captain and rival to [[Alex|Paolo]]. Baldrick is the leader of a band of competent mercenaries on Racinica. He attempted to undertake a venture through [[The Ash]] while guiding some scholars and merchants to [[Baroux]]. During the [[Attack on Blackport]], his mercenary outfit was decimated and he was gravely injured.

[[Alex|Paolo]] found him and treated his injuries after the attack and he has since joined Paolo's Pretty Pirates and a permeant member.
