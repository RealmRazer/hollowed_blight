# Background
A [[Orcs|Orc]] residing in [[Pavlon]] who handled some of the day to day operations at the Mercantile Quarter. He was approached by [[Alex|Paolo]] to trade for equipment, which he agreed to with the payment being a duel.

After losing their bout, Urbul agreed to become on the Pretty Pirates.
