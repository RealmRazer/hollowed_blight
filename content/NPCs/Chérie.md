# Background
An [[Roden]] thief and one of two faces of the Soft Sand Nest in [[Trord]].  Chérie is a point of contact when trying to reach one of the many underworlds in the [[Free Ports of Trord]], and also a companion of [[Blue|Jeremiah]]. She carries herself with confidence and enjoys poking fun at her acquaintances.
