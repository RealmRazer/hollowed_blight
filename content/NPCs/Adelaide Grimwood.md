# Background
Once [[Alex|Paolo's]] best friend, Adelaide is pirate known to cripple merchant ships sailing the open seas of Klei.

Their relationship was strained and broken after [[Alex|Paolo]] turned away from pirate life to become a mercenary.

She assumes a high level position among various pirate groups in [[Trord]], captaining her own ship, but also an alley to the pirate [[Elwig|Captain Elwig]].
