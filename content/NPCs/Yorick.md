# Background
A unknown man who was trapped in the deep sections of the Goldtor mines. He has trouble with his memory and recalls very little of his original mission except for faint recollections of [[Kethic Tamrin|Count Tamrin]]. No one else seemed to survive from his group save some for some mummified bodies and disparate bones.

[[Adam|Father Octavian]] has inducted him to be a member of the [[Numeran Faith|Numeran]] faith, though he seems to have an aversion to his divine light. 

[[Thomas|Cor Devlin]] peers into his being to fight a seed of demonic energy on his soul, through a dangerous operation he was freed from the blight, but escaped into the wilderness in fear of the situation.
