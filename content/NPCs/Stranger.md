# Background
A unknown man who was trapped in the deep sections of the Goldtor mines. He has trouble with his memory and recalls very little of his original mission except for faint recollections of [[Kethic Tamrin|Count Tamrin]]. No one else seemed to survive from his group save some for some mummified bodies and disparate bones.

[[Adam|Father Octavian]] has inducted him to be a member of the [[Numera|Numeran]] faith, though he seems to have an aversion to his divine light.
