# Background
An old [[Elves|Elf]], and the leader of the Golden Accord trade union. He has lived for countless years through ages of war and strife, carrying a hatred in his heart for Mages and [[Magic]], especially for those from [[Anthioc|The Tower]] in [[Trord]].

He previously belonged to an Elvish clan called Firvale, but has long since forgotten and discarded the name. During his time wandering the lands during the ages of war, he was a steward and military defender.
