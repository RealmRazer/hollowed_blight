# Background
A reformed pirate turned mercenary captain. Paolo is attempting to great the largest mercenary band in Racinica.

While traveling with [[The Expedition]], they encountered multiple strong warriors and compatriots that were recruited into The Pretty Pirates. Alongside former competitors and multiple mercenaries from across the carious races, Paolo fought to seal [[Jaggeth]] along with the party and escaped [[Apstarris]].

After returning from the mountain, Paolo used both the pirates they recruited as well as some of the merchant and noble contacts to spread the name of the mercenary company far and wide. Paolo now leads one of the largest merchant and security companies found throughout the land.
