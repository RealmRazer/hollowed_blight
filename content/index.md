---
title: Hollowed Blight
---
 **Current Date**: Illuustio 15, NL 223
 
Trouble at the docks leads to opportunities and new dangers. [[Thomas|Cor Devlin]] finds himself captured by a band of rouges attacking a High Lord from [[Anthioc]]. After securing an arcane key from the mages for use in breaking into the tower, [[Thomas|Cor]] tries to lessen any retaliation to the [[Free Ports of Trord]] by saving the life of [[Bannon Cromwell]]. The danger to the lord passed, he now finds himself trying to get out of their clutches along with his companions.
