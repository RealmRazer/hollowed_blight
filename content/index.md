title: Hollowed Blight
---
 **Current Date**: Illuustio 10, NL 223
 
The Expedition lands in [[Maelbreak]] in search of a [[Ritual Stones| Ritual Stone]]. What welcomes them in the capitol is a looming sense of dread, uncertainty, and mistrust from the populace. The aftermath of a royal assassination attempt and military crack down have left the capitol reeling.

Now The Expedition centers in the home mansion of [[Adam|Father Octavian Pilgrimheart]] to search for their mark and continue their journey.
