# Description
A tri-peak mountain that resides at the heart of [[The Ash]]. The mountain was the initial resting place of the [[Demons|Demon]] [[Jaggeth]], and the inception point of the spreading malady on [[Racinica]].

The mountain goes by many names depending on who asks: the Welkin Hollow, Dusk Spine, The Teeth, or the Gray Peaks.
