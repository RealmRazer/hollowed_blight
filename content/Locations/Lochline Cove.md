# Description
A haven for pirates hidden between a bevy of rocks and other natural geography. The small hub is both a trading place and neutral ground for the bandits of the high seas, hosting a wealth of rare items.

Due to the dangerous nature and waters around the cove, only skilled sailors chance to make their way towards the cove, and few can move to subjugate it.
