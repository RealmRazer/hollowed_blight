# Description
A large fortress in the mountains of [[Otia]], Emeraldcrag spans the middle of the continent, though the central battlement is the only true namesake. Over multiple years, the head of the fortress has amassed  control by integrating many of the surrounding families into the fold.

This consolidation of power makes them a strong pillar of society on [[Otia]], but also leads to underhanded power struggles and difficulty in management that prevents them from being recognized as the seat of power.

_Architecture_: 
Columns of smithed stone line the streets of Emeraldcrag. As situated deep in the mountains, buildings and defenses tower into the sky, both as a show of power, but also to ensure that any person can find trace a path to one of the high spired towers in each district. 

_Layout_: 
The massive central castle is used to break apart Emeraldcrag in to different districts, each one overlooked by members of the different families who have been incorporated. At current, the families of interest in Emeraldcrag are:
* Emeraldcrag
* Ironmountain
* Mythriltor
* Shalemine
* Marblemesa
