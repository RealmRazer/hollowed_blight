# Description
The [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] Capitol of [[Otia]], situation between multiple large mountain passes. Situated at the back of the pass, is the large castle fortress, used as both a meeting place for a number of the high houses across the continent in times of crisis.

Situated in the surrounding town, many smaller [[Dwarves|Dwarven]] clans and banner man trade the fortunes from their expeditions both within and without [[Otia]].
